Who we are

This connector is operated by HDS (contact: info@hdssr.com), for its own internal use. We are the controller of the data described below.

What this connector is

It is a private integration with QuickBooks Online. It is used only by our own employees. It is not offered to customers, not published in any app store, and no one outside our organisation can connect to it or use it.

What data it accesses

Once an authorised employee connects our QuickBooks Online company, the connector can read and, for a limited set of operations, update accounting data in that company. In practice this concerns:

  • customer and supplier records, including name, address, email, telephone and tax registration number;
  • invoices, bills and payment status;
  • accounts, journals and standard financial reports.

The connector does not access bank credentials, card numbers, or payroll data, and it does not initiate any payment.

What we store, and for how long

We deliberately keep storage minimal:

  • Access tokens for the QuickBooks connection, on our server, so the connection does not have to be re-authorised for every request. Removed when the connection is disconnected.
  • An audit log recording which operation was performed, on which record, by which employee, and when. This is a control measure: it lets us answer who changed what. It contains identifiers and the type of operation, not full copies of accounting records.
  • Technical logs of errors, including the identifier that Intuit returns with a response, so that problems can be traced. Retained for a limited period for troubleshooting.

We do not keep a standing copy of the QuickBooks accounting records on our own systems. Data retrieved for a request is used to answer that request.

Who the data is shared with

Two parties are involved, and no others:

  • Anthropic. The connector exposes its operations to Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. When an employee asks a question, the data returned by the connector is processed by Anthropic in order to answer within that conversation. We do not use QuickBooks data to train any model.
  • Amazon Web Services. Our server runs in the AWS region us-east-1, in the United States. AWS hosts the infrastructure; it does not process the content on our behalf for any other purpose.

We do not sell data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not disclose it to any other third party except where we are legally required to.

How access is controlled

The connector sits behind our company single sign-on. Only employees who are members of a dedicated group can reach it. Every action is attributed to the individual who performed it. Client credentials for the QuickBooks connection are stored as environment variables on the server and never appear in source code or in a code repository.

Your choices

The QuickBooks connection can be disconnected at any time from within QuickBooks Online or via the connector itself. Disconnecting removes our access tokens and ends any further access. If you are an employee and you want to know what is recorded about your use of the connector, or want it corrected or removed, write to info@hdssr.com.

Changes

If the way the connector handles data changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
