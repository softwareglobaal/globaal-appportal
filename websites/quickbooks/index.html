QuickBooks connector

A private integration between QuickBooks Online and our internal tooling. Not a public product and not available outside our organisation.

The connector lets our own staff read and update accounting data in our QuickBooks Online company from the tools they already work in. It runs on our own server and is reachable only after signing in with our company single sign-on.
